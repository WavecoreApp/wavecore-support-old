Wavecore Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 11, 2026

Wavecore is designed around working with your music library in a clear and focused way. This page explains, in general terms, how privacy is handled for the app and where to contact support if you have questions.

Information and Content

Wavecore works with music files and related metadata that you choose to import into the app. This can include file names, artwork, titles, and other tag information connected to your audio library.

How Information Is Used

Imported files and metadata are used to provide core app functionality such as library management, playback, tag editing, artwork updates, and export of your library content.

Data Sharing

Please refer to the App Store listing and the app's current privacy details for the most accurate information about data collection and sharing. If you have a specific privacy question, contact support directly.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Wavecore privacy practices, contact wavecoresupport@gmail.com.
